THE MISSION

Face Goliath.

Everyone saw a giant. David saw a challenge that could be faced with God’s help. David was a young shepherd, not a trained soldier. Armed with a sling and five smooth stones, he stepped forward to face Goliath. And he wasn’t alone. David trusted God.

HIS SUPERPOWER
COURAGE
Courage means:
  • ⭐ Being brave doesn’t mean you’re never afraid.
  • ⭐ It means trusting God and doing what is right—even when something looks impossible.
POWER VERSE
“The battle is the LORD’S.”
1 Samuel 17:47
DID YOU KNOW?
  • 🐑 David was a shepherd.
  • 🪕 He played the harp.
  • 🪨 He defeated Goliath with a sling and a stone.
  • 👑 David later became king of Israel.
  • 📜 He is traditionally associated with many of the Psalms.
YOUR HERO MISSION
What is one “giant” you are facing?
It could be:
Take courage. Face it with faith.
★
DAVID’S POWER: BE BRAVE.
You don’t have to be the biggest to make a big difference. 🔆

THE MISSION

Speak up for her people.

Esther was queen — but her people were in danger. She could have stayed silent. Instead, she chose to approach the king and ask him to save her people. It was risky. It was frightening. But Esther decided that doing the right thing was more important than being comfortable.

HER SUPERPOWER
BRAVERY
Sometimes being a hero means:
  • ⭐ Speaking up.
  • ⭐ Standing up.
  • ⭐ Doing the right thing.
POWER VERSE
“Who knows whether you have come to the kingdom for such a time as this?”
Esther 4:14
DID YOU KNOW?
  • 👑 Esther became queen.
  • 📜 Her Hebrew name was Hadassah.
  • 👥 She was raised by her cousin Mordecai.
  • 🕎 Her story is connected with the Jewish festival of Purim.
YOUR HERO MISSION
See someone being treated unfairly? You can be a hero.
★
ESTHER’S POWER: USE YOUR VOICE.
Sometimes one brave voice can make a big difference. 🔆

THE MISSION

Lead God’s people to freedom.

Moses was chosen to lead the Israelites out of slavery in Egypt. But their journey wasn’t easy. When they reached the Red Sea, there seemed to be nowhere to go. Moses trusted God. He raised his staff — and the sea opened. The Israelites walked across on dry ground.

HIS SUPERPOWER
LEADERSHIP
A great leader doesn’t always know what will happen next. A great leader:
  • ⭐ Trusts.
  • ⭐ Stays calm.
  • ⭐ Moves forward.
  • ⭐ Helps others move forward too.
POWER VERSE
“The LORD shall fight for you.”
Exodus 14:14
DID YOU KNOW?
  • 🔥 God spoke to Moses through a burning bush.
  • 🌊 Moses led the Israelites through the Red Sea.
  • 📜 He received the Ten Commandments.
  • 🧺 Moses was placed in a basket as a baby.
YOUR HERO MISSION
Someone around you needs help. Can you be the person who says:
“Come on. I’ll help you.”
★
MOSES’ POWER: LEAD THE WAY.
A leader helps others find their way forward. 🔆

THE MISSION

Stay faithful — even under pressure.

Some people were jealous of Daniel. They created a law to stop him from praying to God. But Daniel didn’t change who he was. He continued to pray. For this, he was thrown into a den of lions. But God protected him.

HIS SUPERPOWER
FAITH
Faith means:
  • ⭐ Trusting God.
  • ⭐ Doing what is right.
  • ⭐ Staying strong when others pressure you.
POWER VERSE
“My God sent his angel, and he shut the lions’ mouths.”
Daniel 6:22
DID YOU KNOW?
  • 🦁 Daniel survived a den of lions.
  • 🙏 He prayed three times each day.
  • 👑 He served under several kings.
  • 🧠 He was known for his wisdom and ability to interpret dreams.
YOUR HERO MISSION
What would you do if everyone around you said, “Don’t do the right thing”?
Remember Daniel. Stand strong.
★
DANIEL’S POWER: STAY FAITHFUL.
Do the right thing — even when nobody else is doing it. 🔆

THE MISSION

Build the ark.

God gave Noah a huge job. Build an ark. Gather his family. Bring the animals. Prepare for the flood. Noah didn’t know exactly what would happen. But he trusted God and followed His instructions.

HIS SUPERPOWER
OBEDIENCE
Obedience means:
  • ✔ Listening.
  • ✔ Trusting.
  • ✔ Doing what is right.
  • ✔ Even when you don’t understand everything.
POWER VERSE
“Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him.”
Genesis 6:22
DID YOU KNOW?
  • 🚢 The ark had three levels.
  • 📏 Genesis gives measurements for the ark.
  • 🐘 Animals came aboard the ark.
  • 🌈 The rainbow became a sign of God’s covenant.
  • 👨‍👩‍👦 Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham and Japheth.
YOUR HERO MISSION
Your parents ask you to do something. Your teacher asks you to do something. God’s Word teaches you something. What will you do?
★
NOAH’S POWER: DO THE RIGHT THING.
Even when you don’t understand the whole plan. 🔆

THE MISSION

Don’t give up on someone you love.

Ruth lost her husband. Her mother-in-law Naomi had also lost her husband and sons. Both could have returned to their own home. Instead, Ruth chose to stay with Naomi. She traveled with her to Bethlehem and worked hard to provide for them. Her loyalty changed both their lives.

HER SUPERPOWER
LOYALTY
Loyalty means:
  • ⭐ Standing by someone in hard times.
  • ⭐ Showing kindness.
  • ⭐ Choosing to do what is right.
  • ⭐ Caring for someone, even when it is difficult.
POWER VERSE
“Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will be my people and your God my God.”
Ruth 1:16
DID YOU KNOW?
  • 🌾 Ruth gathered leftover grain from the fields.
  • 🤲 She showed kindness and hard work.
  • 👥 Ruth became part of the family line of King David.
  • 👑 Ruth was a Moabite who became part of God’s story.
YOUR HERO MISSION
Think of someone who needs a little kindness today. What can YOU do?
★
RUTH’S POWER: DON’T GIVE UP ON PEOPLE.
Sometimes your kindness can change someone’s whole story. 🔆
